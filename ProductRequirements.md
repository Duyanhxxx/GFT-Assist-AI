# Product Requirements

## Product Vision

GFT-Assist-AI is a SaaS support operations platform that combines ticket orchestration, grounded AI assistance, and human escalation. The product is designed for teams that need faster response times without losing control, auditability, or answer quality.

## Problem Statement

Support teams lose time on repetitive classification, duplicate cases, noisy spam, inconsistent prioritization, and manual knowledge lookup. Traditional chatbots answer too aggressively, fail silently, and offer little traceability. The platform must behave like an accountable AI agent system instead of an always-answering bot.

## Target Users

- Admin: configures policies, AI behavior, access control, and reporting
- Operator: reviews escalations, manages tickets, curates knowledge, overrides AI decisions
- Viewer: monitors dashboards, logs, and outcomes without operational write access
- Customer: submits support requests and receives grounded responses or follow-up questions

## Core Outcomes

- Reduce manual triage workload
- Improve first-response speed
- Increase resolution quality with grounded answers
- Route urgent or uncertain cases to humans quickly
- Provide explainable, auditable AI behavior

## Product Scope

- Multi-role internal web application
- Customer ticket intake workflow
- AI triage and knowledge-grounded response generation
- Human escalation and override workflow
- Knowledge ingestion and semantic retrieval
- Monitoring, analytics, and AI audit logging
- Production deployment and engineering documentation

## Success Metrics

- AI resolution rate
- Escalation accuracy
- Average response time
- Duplicate detection precision
- Spam detection precision
- Confidence calibration quality
- Knowledge retrieval hit rate
- Human override rate
- Cost per resolved ticket
